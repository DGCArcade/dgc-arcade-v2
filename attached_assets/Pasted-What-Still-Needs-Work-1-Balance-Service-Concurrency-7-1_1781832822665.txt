What Still Needs Work
1. Balance Service Concurrency (7/10)

This is probably the biggest backend risk I found.

Inside:

deductBalance()

I see:

get current state
calculate
update

That pattern is vulnerable if two actions happen simultaneously.

Example:

Bet
Withdrawal

at the exact same moment.

I'd tell Claude:

Audit balance-service.ts for race conditions and move critical balance operations into database transactions with row-level locking.
