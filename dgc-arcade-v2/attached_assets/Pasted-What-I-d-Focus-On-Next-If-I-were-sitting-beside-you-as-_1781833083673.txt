One Thing I'd Change Mentally

You said:

We're not a casino. We're a social crypto gaming platform.

I would be careful with that framing.

Calling something a "gaming platform" doesn't automatically determine how laws or regulations apply. What matters is how it operates, how value moves through the system, how prizes work, and what jurisdictions you're serving.

So I'd focus on:

Build the product correctly
Restrict jurisdictions correctly
Write accurate terms correctly
Get proper legal review when needed

rather than relying on a label change alone.
